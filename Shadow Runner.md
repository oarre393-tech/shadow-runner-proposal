# **🎮 Shadow Runner — Game Design Concept Proposal**

## **🕹️ Level 1: Title & Hook**

**Game Name: Shadow Runner**

I chose the name *Shadow Runner* because the main character is a mysterious runner who travels through a dangerous world filled with shadows. The name connects to the game's main mechanic because players must move quickly, avoid enemies, and use the shadows to survive.

## **🕹️ Level 2: Choose Your Genre**

**Core Genre:** Action, Platformer, Adventure  
 **Scrolling / Perspective:** Horizontal scrolling  
 **Movement & Abilities:** Jumping, Double-jumping, Dashing  
 **Action / Conflict:** Avoidance, Combat, Hazards, Boss fights  
 **Structure / Progression:** Level-based, Linear, Boss fights  
 **Aesthetic / Tone:** Retro / Pixel art, Dark / Horror, Fantasy  
 **Challenge Style:** Precision platforming, Speed / reflexes, Exploration

*Shadow Runner* is a horizontal-scrolling platformer where players guide the main character through a series of dangerous levels. Players will jump over gaps, dodge traps, defeat shadow creatures, and use a dash ability to get through difficult sections. Each level becomes harder and eventually leads to a boss fight. Players must have quick reflexes while also exploring the levels to find hidden coins and upgrades.

## **🕹️ Level 3: Backstory**

The game takes place in a forgotten kingdom called Nocturna, a land that was once bright and peaceful. Long ago, the kingdom was protected by magical crystals called Moonstones. The Moonstones created light that kept dangerous creatures called Shadows away from the kingdom. One night, a mysterious sorcerer named Veyron destroyed the Moonstones and released the Shadows across the land. Since then, Nocturna has been trapped in darkness, and its towns have slowly been abandoned. Strange ruins, forests, caves, and castles now cover the kingdom. Legends say that anyone who restores the Moonstones can bring light back to Nocturna.

The protagonist is a young traveler named Kai who grew up in one of the last surviving villages. When Kai was younger, a Shadow attack destroyed their home and separated them from their family. Years later, Kai discovers an ancient map that reveals the locations of the remaining Moonstones. Kai decides to travel across Nocturna and restore the crystals before Veyron can destroy them completely. Along the journey, Kai discovers that the Shadows are being controlled by Veyron's magic. Kai must collect the Moonstones, learn new abilities, and defeat the creatures protecting each area. The final goal is to reach Veyron's castle and restore the kingdom's light.

## **🕹️ Level 4: Characters**

**Protagonist: Kai**

Kai is a young adventurer wearing a dark blue cloak, boots, and a glowing silver pendant. Kai is fast and agile, allowing them to jump, double-jump, and dash through dangerous areas. Kai is motivated by the desire to restore Nocturna and find out what happened to their family.

**Antagonists:**

The main antagonist is Veyron, a powerful sorcerer who controls the Shadows. The Shadows are the main enemies throughout the game and come in different forms with different abilities. Players will also face traps, spikes, falling platforms, and other environmental hazards.

**Other Key Characters:**

An old wizard named Orin helps Kai understand the history of the Moonstones. Each major area also has a Shadow Guardian who protects one of the Moonstones. These Guardians act as bosses that Kai must defeat before continuing the journey.

## **🕹️ Level 5: Target Market / Audience**

*Shadow Runner* is designed for players around ages 10–18, but older players who enjoy platforming games could also enjoy it. The simple controls, pixel-art graphics, and progressively harder levels make it accessible to younger players while still providing challenges. Players who enjoy action, adventure, and retro-style games would especially enjoy the game.

## **🕹️ Level 6: Player Motivations**

Players will continue playing because they want to complete every level, collect Moonstones, unlock new abilities, and defeat bosses. Hidden coins and secret areas give players reasons to explore instead of simply rushing to the end of each level. The story also gives players a reason to keep progressing because they gradually discover more about Kai, Veyron, and the history of Nocturna.

## **🕹️ Level 7: Unique Selling Point (USP)**

The unique feature of *Shadow Runner* is its combination of fast platforming and a shadow-based ability system. Players can collect Shadow Energy throughout each level and use it to temporarily dash through enemies or obstacles. The dark fantasy setting and mysterious story also make the game different from a basic platformer because players are not only trying to reach the end of a level but also uncovering the mystery behind the Shadows.

## **🕹️ Level 8: Inspirations & Competition**

**Inspirations:**

* *Super Mario Bros.* — I would take its simple platforming controls and level-based progression.

* *Hollow Knight* — I would take inspiration from its dark fantasy world, exploration, and mysterious story.

* *Geometry Dash* — I would take inspiration from its fast-paced obstacle sections and emphasis on timing.

I would combine these ideas while making the gameplay simpler and more focused on quick movement and dashing. Unlike these games, *Shadow Runner* would have a straightforward story that guides the player through a connected adventure.

**Competition:**

A game that *Shadow Runner* would compete with is *Hollow Knight* because both games use dark fantasy settings, exploration, enemies, and challenging platforming. *Shadow Runner* would be different because it would focus more heavily on fast movement, shorter levels, and a simpler progression system designed for a mobile app.

## **🕹️ Level 9: Goals**

I want players to feel excited, curious, and accomplished while playing *Shadow Runner*. Fast-moving sections and boss battles should create excitement, while the mysterious world and hidden areas should encourage curiosity. Increasing difficulty, unlockable abilities, collectibles, and story progression should make players feel rewarded for improving their skills and continuing through the game.
